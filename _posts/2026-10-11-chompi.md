Discontinued commercial sampler released as open source under MIT license. Built around the Electrosmith Daisy Seed with a second PCM3060 codec, LiPo battery charger, USB-C, microSD, built-in MEMS microphone.

Three firmwares are available: TAPE (the stock sampler), TEMPO (pattern generator) and WAVE (wavetable synth, also a good starting point for writing your own firmware).
